About

The person behind the page reviews

I’m ABDU’LLAH SHEDA, a book marketing specialist who helps authors get found.

Writing the book is half the work. The other half is making sure the right readers can find it, trust the page and buy. I work directly with self-published and traditionally published authors, across fiction and non-fiction.

My method is simple: read the page the way a browsing reader does, understand the genre, and fix what stands between the book and more readers. I keep my messages short, my advice specific and my promises honest.

Plain language

No jargon walls. You’ll know what I changed and why.

Genre first

A romance reader and a business reader shop differently, so each plan is built for its reader.

Evidence before promises

I won’t promise sales or reviews. I promise careful work I control.

Your assets stay yours

Your pages, accounts and reader lists belong to you.

Best fit

  • Authors with a published or upcoming book
  • Authors open to testing and learning from data
  • Authors who want clear reports

Not a fit

  • Guaranteed rank or sales
  • Fake reviews or review swaps
  • Editing or ghostwriting

How I read an Amazon page

  1. The cover and title at thumbnail size
  2. The first line of the blurb, then its ending
  3. Categories, subtitle and visible keywords
  4. Series links, reviews, A+ Content and the author page
  5. Price, formats and what similar books are doing

For fiction

Tropes, mood, series order and read-through matter most. I focus on the hook, the category fit and reader communities such as BookTok.

For non-fiction

The promise, the reader it helps and your credibility matter most. I focus on the subtitle, the outcome and a reader magnet for your list.

    Launch timeline checklist

    A typical plan. Every book is different, so adjust it to your release date.

    Settle your categories and keywords, finalize the cover and subtitle, and plan how your series will link together.

    Start building your advance reader list, draft your launch emails and prepare a reader magnet.

    Set up the pre-order or listing, finish the blurb and A+ Content, and schedule any promotions.

    Send advance copies with a friendly reminder to leave an honest review, and prepare graphics, trailer and ad creative.

    Send the launch email, switch on your ads, share on social media and check results daily.

    Review what worked, adjust keywords and ads, thank your readers and plan the next promotion.

    Glossary

    Terms authors often hear.

    ARC

    An advance reader copy given before release so readers can review it honestly.

    BSR

    Best Sellers Rank, Amazon’s sales rank. A lower number means better recent sales.

    A+ Content

    The rich section on an Amazon page with images and text below the blurb.

    KDP

    Kindle Direct Publishing, Amazon’s self-publishing platform.

    Kindle Unlimited

    Amazon’s reading subscription, which pays authors per page read.

    ACOS

    Advertising cost of sales: ad spend divided by the sales those ads produced.

    ROAS

    Return on ad spend: how much revenue each dollar of ads brings back.

    Reader magnet

    A free bonus given in exchange for an email address.

    Backend keywords

    The seven hidden keyword slots in your KDP book details.

    Listopia

    Goodreads’ user-voted book lists.

    Editorial Reviews

    An Amazon page section for praise and quotes about the book.

    Trope

    A recurring story pattern that readers search for, such as enemies to lovers.
